Research

CFD and thermofluids research at AASML — AUST Applied Simulation and Modeling Lab, with manuscripts in preparation for peer-reviewed journals and conferences. Every study is anchored to a validation benchmark before new geometry is explored.

FLOW PHYSICS · CFD–DPM

Turbulent particle deposition in pipes

Reproduced the benchmark aerosol deposition study of Shuvo et al. (2022) with an Eulerian–Lagrangian Discrete Phase Model in ANSYS Fluent 2024 R2, capturing deposition efficiency vs. Stokes number to within ~2.6% of published results. Extended validation across ten particle diameters and Re = 5,000–10,000, resolving the turbophoretic transport that drives particles toward the wall.

// ~2.6% avg deviation · Re 5k–10k · manuscript in prep

AASML · NEXTGEN ENERGY SYSTEMS

Hypersonic re-entry (CEV) aerodynamics

CFD of a hypersonic Crew Exploration Vehicle re-entry capsule using a density-based compressible solver in ANSYS Fluent (SST k–ω, Roe-FDS flux, Sutherland viscosity). Extracting mean shock stand-off distance vs. Mach number across capsule geometries, and characterizing bow-shock formation ahead of the heat shield with wake vortex structures.

// density-based · manuscript in prep

CFD FRAMEWORK

High-fidelity NACA 0012 simulation

Validated ANSYS Fluent framework (k–ω SST) for aerodynamic loading at Re = 3×10⁶. Structured C-grid with near-wall refinement (y⁺ < 1) and a grid-independence study; predicted CL, CD, L/D across 0–20° AoA, validated against NASA experimental data.

AERODYNAMICS · Mach > 1

Transonic rocket fin optimization

Investigated compressibility effects and high-speed aerodynamic performance. Compared trapezoidal, swept, and high-aspect-ratio fin geometries under controlled conditions, showing ~33% drag reduction with swept configurations.

AERODYNAMICS

NACA 23012 airfoil optimization

Parametric study varying camber, thickness, and leading-edge geometry at Re = 2×10⁶, balancing lift, drag, and structural constraints. Automated sweeps in XFOIL/XFLR5 achieved a 6.49% L/D improvement.
