About

I'm a mechanical design engineer working at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics, aerodynamics, and precision CAD. My day-to-day at Palki Motors is mechanical design and CAD reverse-engineering across vehicle R&D programs; my research life at AASML is CFD — particle deposition, hypersonic re-entry aerodynamics, and heat-transfer enhancement.

What ties it together is a habit of not trusting a simulation until it matches something real — a NASA data set, a published paper, or a physical part in my hand. That validation-first mindset is what I bring to every model.

Research

CFD and aerospace work at AASML (Ahsanullah University of Science & Technology), aimed at publication and conference presentation. Every study is anchored to a validation benchmark before any new geometry is explored.

CFD · DPM

Particle deposition in pipe flow

Lagrangian discrete-phase modelling of anthracite particle deposition in turbulent pipe flow, validated against Shuvo et al. (2022) in ANSYS Fluent with RNG k-ε and enhanced wall treatment. Found Reynolds number acts as an independent deposition parameter below St ≈ 0.4.

// ~2.6% avg deviation vs. published benchmark

ANSYS FluentRNG k-εLagrangian DPM
HYPERSONICS

Re-entry capsule aerodynamics

Shock stand-off distance for a CEV-type re-entry capsule validated against Juhany et al. (2024) across Mach 1.4–7, with a heat-flux extraction workflow established in CFD-Post for high-enthalpy surface loading.

// ~9% avg SSD deviation, Mach 1.4–7
